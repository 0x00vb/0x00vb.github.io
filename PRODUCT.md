# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Engineers looking for clear technical explanations, and collaborators assessing Valentino's engineering judgment. Readers arrive from a shared article or browse the complete archive.

## Product Purpose
A personal technical blog and future portfolio. Markdown is the source of truth. The homepage presents all writing and a brief introduction. Subjects include security, systems, design, GPU computing, ML, agentic AI, and personal essays.

## Brand Personality
Personal, precise, curious. The user's reference is alexine.rip: an independent personal address with a distinct visual identity. Calm light mode and an equally deliberate dark mode.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS cards, fake popularity, invented biography, exaggerated expertise claims, canned AI prose, em dashes, decorative dashboards, animation that delays reading.

## Design Principles
Show reasoning through specific articles. Give breadth a small number of clear subjects. Make the archive the entrance. Keep opinion drafts distinguishable from verified personal history. Preserve static HTML portability for GitHub Pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive text, reduced motion, nonvisual explanations of diagrams, equivalent list access to graph content.
